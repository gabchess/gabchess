# Gabriel Abreu

Product marketer and GTM engineer. I build the GTM systems behind the story: analytics stacks, evaluation pipelines, content engines, and the Solana programs underneath them. 15 years in marketing, the last five in crypto and infrastructure.

## Systems and programs

**[hedwig-sol](https://github.com/gabchess/hedwig-sol)**: one canonical role store that Anchor programs share, so teams stop rebuilding access control in every program. 6 instructions, 21 LiteSVM integration tests, a documented threat model. Built on a Solana Foundation grant.

**[worldcup-pari-market](https://github.com/gabchess/worldcup-pari-market)**: World Cup prediction markets where settlement follows a predefined onchain proof path, with no operator, committee, or token vote. Live on devnet with finalized lifecycle transactions and 39 Rust tests. [Demo](https://youtu.be/2Vh6RPLNd-U).

**[walletbrief-monad](https://github.com/gabchess/walletbrief-monad)**: persistent Monad wallet briefs with human-approved, revoke-only EIP-7702 execution.

**[kageb](https://github.com/gabchess/kageb)**: private intent pooling for Solana that folds four orders into one aggregate trade.

**[silvia](https://github.com/gabchess/silvia)**: a confirmation-first ordering assistant for older adults. Chat instead of learning a new app; 50+ users completed an order with it.

Off GitHub: a six-agent AI system runs my daily marketing operations (research, drafts, review, planning), with a human sign-off at every step. It held quality above threshold in 27 of 30 content cycles, and its daily market brief hit 97% accuracy across 31 traced runs.
